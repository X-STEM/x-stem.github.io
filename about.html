What is XSTEM?

Building tomorrow's entrepreneurs today.

XSTEM is a premier club where students work collaboratively to create their own startup companies and compete to win prizes at the regional, national, and international level.

Through this club, students will learn a rigorous MIT-based curriculum to develop their companies, create comprehensive business plans, and gain valuable knowledge from experienced mentors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior business experience to join?
Not at all! XSTEM is open to students of all experience levels. Our curriculum is designed to teach you everything from the ground up.
What are the attendance requirements?
To maintain active member status and receive club credit, you must attend at least 50% of our general meetings and participate in our mandatory LaunchSB event.
Can I join a competition team if I don't have an idea?
Yes! We host brainstorming sessions and team-building events early in the year to help members find co-founders and develop strong startup ideas together.
Is there a membership fee?
There is no initial membership fee to join the club, but some specific competitions may require small registration fees.
